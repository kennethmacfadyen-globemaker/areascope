    Questions people ask

    Where does the data come from?

    Four official/open sources, fetched live at the moment you search: street-level crime from data.police.uk, geography and deprivation statistics from postcodes.io (ONS data), live flood alerts from the Environment Agency, and amenities from OpenStreetMap. The report shows exactly what the public record shows — nothing is edited or embellished.

    Does it cover the whole UK?

    Amenities, transport and the area profile work UK-wide. Street-level crime covers England, Wales and Northern Ireland — Police Scotland and Greater Manchester Police don't publish to the open API, so in those areas the score is calculated without the crime component and the report tells you exactly that. We'd rather show you a gap than a made-up number. Flood alerts cover England.

    How accurate is the crime data?

    It's the same data the police publish, anonymised to street level, usually 1–2 months behind today. It counts reported crime within roughly one mile of the postcode. Treat it as a strong signal, not a verdict — always visit an area in person too.

    Do you store my searches or email?

    No. There's no server, no database, no cookies, no analytics. Your browser talks directly to the public APIs. The £5 unlock is stored only on your own device.

    How does the £5 unlock work?

    Payment goes through Buy Me a Coffee (card / Apple Pay / Google Pay). After paying, you press "I've paid — unlock" and full reports open on your device permanently. It's an honour system — AreaScope is a one-person product and I'd rather trust you than make you create an account.

    Can I use a report in a dispute / with my landlord / for a mortgage?

    Reports are for guidance only. They summarise public data accurately but aren't professional advice or an official document.
